## Federal airport concession rounds 2019-2022 (5th, 6th, 7th) and the returns of Viracopos and Galeao

- Years: 2019, 2021, 2022 (returns 2020-2026).
- Government: Bolsonaro (all three auctions); Lula 3 handles the relicitacoes.
- Transferred: 5th round, 12 airports in 3 blocks; 6th round, 22 airports; 7th round, 11-airport SP-MS-PA-MG block (with Congonhas) plus Norte II plus a General Aviation block.
- Who got it and price vs minimum:
  - 5th round (2019): total R$ 2.377 bn against minimum R$ 219 mn, average agio 986%, investment promise R$ 3.5 bn over 30 years. Nordeste: Aena (Spain) R$ 1.900 bn, agio 1,010.69%, minimum R$ 171 mn. Sudeste: Zurich Airport R$ 437 mn, agio 830.15%, minimum R$ 47 mn. Centro-Oeste: Consorcio Aeroeste R$ 40 mn, agio 4,739%, minimum R$ 0.8 mn. https://www.congressoemfoco.com.br/especial/noticias/leilao-de-privatizacao-12-aeroportos-arrecada-r-237-bilhoes/
  - 6th round (2021): R$ 3.3 bn, agio about 3,822%, expected investment R$ 6.1 bn. CCR: Sul block (9 airports incl. Curitiba, Foz) R$ 2.128 bn, Central block (6 airports) R$ 754 mn. Vinci: Norte R$ 420 mn. Per-block agio differs by source: Sul 1,500% vs 1,534%; Central 9,000% vs 9,156%; Norte 777% vs 778%. https://congressoemfoco.com.br/noticia/14844/com-agio-de-3-800-governo-arrecada-r-33-bilhoes-em-leilao-de-aeroportos
  - 7th round (18 Aug 2022): Aena was the only bidder for the SP-MS-PA-MG block (incl. Congonhas): R$ 2.45 bn, agio 231.02%, 30 years. General Aviation (Campo de Marte, Jacarepagua): XP Infra IV FIP, R$ 141.4 mn, agio 0.01%, no competition. Norte II (Belem, Macapa): Consorcio Novo Norte R$ 125 mn, agio 119.78%. https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/economia/noticia/2022-08/unica-empresa-apresentar-proposta-aena-leva-aeroporto-de-congonhas

- Scale: private concessions carried 77.5% of passengers for 2011-2021, rising to 91.6% with the 7th round. https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/economia/noticia/2022-08/unica-empresa-apresentar-proposta-aena-leva-aeroporto-de-congonhas

## Federal road concessions 2019-2025 (Dutra 2021, 2023-24 balance, relicitacao and repactuacao)

- Years: 2019-2025. Governments: Bolsonaro (Dutra), Lula 3 (2023-25 rounds, repactuacao).
- Dutra / Rio-Santos (29 Oct 2021): CCR won with the maximum 15.31% toll discount; EcoRodovias offered 10.6%. Outorga R$ 1.77 bn, investment about R$ 14.83 bn, 30 years, 625.8 km; only two bidders admitted. https://diariodotransporte.com.br/2021/10/29/ccr-vence-leilao-de-concessao-da-dutra-e-parte-da-rio-santos/
- Legal mechanism: ANTT auctions with toll discount (and outorga) as the bid; relicitacao by Lei 13.448/2017; the 2023 "otimizacao" program (Portaria 848, Aug 2023).
- Ministry's own balance 2023-24: 9 auctions, about 4,300 km, more than R$ 111 bn potential investment; 15 auctions expected in 2025; new policy redirects outorga into works; Parana lot 3 won with about 70% off the average toll; 14 contracts joined the optimization program, TCU approved 3. https://www.tribunapr.com.br/noticias/automoveis/leiloes-rodoviarios-levam-r-111-bi-para-modernizacao-de-estradas/ (Ministry account, not independent).

- After: 2025 cycle of 13 auctions, about 6,200 km, R$ 135 bn [UNVERIFIED, agenciainfra snippet]; first relicitacao under Lei 13.448 on BR-040 in 2024 [UNVERIFIED].

## Petrobras refinery sales 2021-2023 (Reman, Lubnor, SIX, failed Refap/Repar/RNEST)

- Context: the 2019 CADE agreement (TCC) obliged Petrobras to sell 8 refineries. Mataripe/RLAM is already in 06; additions on it are under the next case.

Headline price quoted as US$ 189.5 mn in the eixos article, so the price ranges US$ 189.5-257.2 mn depending on whether adjustments and deposit are counted. - Lubnor (Ceara, 8,000 b/d): sold to Grepar for US$ 34 mn (signed 25 May 2022; US$ 3.4 mn on signing, US$ 9.6 mn at closing, US$ 21 mn deferred). https://eixos.com.br/petrobras-vende-refinaria-lubnor-no-ceara-por-us-34-milhoes/
  - Ineep (union-linked think tank) says market value was at least US$ 62 mn, so the price was 55% below; no method detail and no Petrobras reply in the article. https://www.jb.com.br/economia/2022/05/1037721-refinaria-da-petrobras-e-vendida-por-preco-55-abaixo-do-valor-de-mercado-diz-ineep.html
  - Contract terminated 27 Nov 2023 over unmet conditions precedent about land involving two federal entities; Grepar says it waived them and will sue. Competing asphalt distributors had opposed the sale because of Grepar's link to Greca. Lubnor stays with Petrobras. https://eixos.com.br/petrobras-cancela-venda-da-lubnor/
- SIX (shale unit, Sao Mateus do Sul, PR): sold to Forbes & Manhattan Resources for US$ 33 mn (eixos), closed early Nov 2022 to Forbes Resources Brazil Holding (https://ogj.com/refining-processing/article/14286478/petrobras-completes-reman-refinery-sale).
- Failures: Repar's first sale attempt halted over low offers; RNEST got no proposals (eixos). Ultrapar's reported Refap interest of about US$ 1.2-1.4 bn ended Oct 2021 [UNVERIFIED snippets, Valor via Suno, Money Times]. Ultra could not buy Repar on antitrust grounds [UNVERIFIED].
- Legal mechanism: CADE TCC 2019; sales without a new law, through Petrobras board and binding rounds.
- Who benefits: private buyers gain regional fuel monopolies in small markets; Petrobras (federal and private shareholders) gets cash. Critics (FUP, Ineep) say it moves regional monopolies from public to private hands; defenders (CADE) say it opens competition. The Lubnor and Reman outcomes (one cancelled, one price still being adjusted) show the process was messy.

## Land regularization - additions (hectares)

- Sources: Imazon / Amazonia 2030 report "Regularizacao Fundiaria em Areas Federais na Amazonia Legal" (Brenda Brito, Jun 2022). https://acervo.socioambiental.org/sites/default/files/documents/t1d00112.pdf
- Undesignated land: about 144 million ha, nearly 30% of the Legal Amazon; about 300,000 occupations on federal public land not regularized.
- Law: Lei 11.952/2009 allows no-bid titling up to 2,500 ha for occupations up to Dec 2011. The original cap was 1,500 ha, raised to 2,500 ha by Lei 13.465/2017. Free titling up to 1 fiscal module (100 ha in the Amazon); faster track up to 4 modules (400 ha).
- Terra Legal target: 300,000 properties, 67 million ha, 463 municipalities. Delivered: under 40,000 titles in 2009-2018, about 5,000 requests rejected, average 3,190 titles per year. 753 definitive titles in 2021.
- As of Jan 2021: 85,359 georeferenced untitled parcels covering 11.1 million ha, of which 6.1 million ha (55%) overlapped undesignated federal public forests where titling is prohibited by law. TCU 2020 found 887,000 ha in 5,080 properties with rejected requests and no measures to retake them.
- Incra deliveries: 2016: 17.3k; 2017 about four times; 2018: 78.6k; 2020: over 100k; 2021: 91.7k so far, expected 130k. Titula Brasil launched Feb 2021, goal 130k; about half of roughly 2,000 eligible municipalities joined. The MST opposes the title-deed model; critics say it facilitates concentration. https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/vida-e-cidadania/incra-governo-federal-acelera-concessao-titulos-produtores-rurais-mst-contrario/
- Snippets [UNVERIFIED]: 404,933 titles Jan 2019-Aug 2022 vs 332,818 under Lula and Dilma; about 88% provisional (CCU) per a partisan outlet; federal land without allocation about 60 Mha (CNA) vs more than 50 Mha (MDA).
- Gap: total hectares titled under Lei 13.465 / Terra Legal not found.

## Orcamento secreto (emendas de relator, RP9)

- Years: 2020-2022 (decision Dec 2022); governments: Bolsonaro, with Congress (Lira, Pacheco) controlling indications.
- What: relator amendments (RP9) decided by the budget rapporteur with no set criteria; the visible name is the rapporteur's, not the requesting member. https://www.aosfatos.org/noticias/orcamento-secreto-stf/
- Size, as a range: R$ 53.5 bn authorized 2020-2022 (Siga Brasil via Aos Fatos), of which R$ 45 bn committed and R$ 28.9 bn paid (same page); R$ 45 bn committed and R$ 28 bn paid by Oct 2022 (Outras Palavras, https://outraspalavras.net/outrasmidias/quem-controla-o-orcamento-secreto/); about R$ 80 bn budgeted and R$ 30 bn paid (Conjur, Scaff) [UNVERIFIED snippet]. The Aos Fatos page also shows conflicting annual planned figures (R$ 20.1 bn vs R$ 30 bn for 2020), so use the ranges above.
- Who got it: health was 57.4% of the R$ 28.79 bn paid, then urbanism 11.53%, social assistance 5%, education 4.68%; six parties (PP, PL, PSD, MDB, Uniao, Republicanos) hold 81% of the 2022 executed amount attributed to party indications; external users (sponsored non-members) controlled about 31%. Lira tops the 2022 list (R$ 134.5 mn indicated). An NGO (ICA) received R$ 98 mn in 2020-2022. Covers Jan-Oct 2022 only and manual matching, so partial. Same Outras Palavras page. Another report found the PL, PP and Republicanos took R$ 2.86 bn in 2020-2021 (not R$ 28 bn as a snippet headline suggested). https://www.correiobraziliense.com.br/politica/2022/05/amp/5006963-centrao-abocanha-rs-28-bi-das-emendas-do-orcamento-secreto.html
- Legal: Rosa Weber suspended execution Nov 2021 (8-2 upheld); on 19 Dec 2022 the STF ruled RP9 unconstitutional 6-5 (ADPFs 850, 851, 854, 1014). The 2023 budget reserved R$ 19.4 bn for relator amendments, and Congress passed a stopgap resolution days before the ruling. https://www.infomoney.com.br/?p=2011420
- After: merits decision 19 Dec 2024 declared the practices incompatible with the Constitution; RP8/RP9 execution partly resumed; R$ 12.6 bn of unpaid items paid in 2025 [UNVERIFIED snippets; Senate note was unreadable binary].
- Counter-view: Congress argues the amendments are its legitimate budget prerogative; the criticism is lack of traceability, not that congressional amendments exist.
- Link to the theme: this is a channel where public budget flowed to private contractors through politically picked recipients, not a sale of an asset.

## Patterns

- Low minimum prices and huge agios (airports) are read two ways: low reserve prices by the state, or risk pricing by bidders. No independent valuation was opened.
- Hand-backs and renegotiation followed in airports and roads (Viracopos, Galeao, 16 of 24 roads), so risk returned to the public side.
- Petrobras divestment partly reverses: Lubnor cancelled, Bahia Terra dropped, Carmopolis default, Mataripe pricing under CADE review.
- Several mega-prices are disputed only by labor-aligned sources (Ineep, FUP, OSP); the claims need a neutral valuation before use.
- Congress-controlled money (RP9, Codevasf) routes public budget to private contractors with low traceability; proven loss figures are small, the volume is large.
- Opaque control changes (Braskem) happen with no cash, through debt-for-equity structures, with minority shareholders taking the risk.
- Many "who financed" fields remain unfilled because bidders' financing is rarely published.
