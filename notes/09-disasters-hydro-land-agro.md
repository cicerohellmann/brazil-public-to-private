# Brazil: dams, infrastructure, agribusiness, land conflicts, sanitation and urban disasters

Scope: hydro and water dams, large infrastructure, agribusiness and pesticides, land conflicts and massacres, urban floods and landslides. Mining and oil/chemical accidents are excluded (other agents).

Newest first. Each entry lists ownership at the time, deaths, displacement, environmental damage, legal outcome, and sources. Where sources disagree, the range is given and the disagreement is named. "Not found" means my searches did not return a figure. Nothing here is estimated by me.

Ownership key: **State** = state-owned company or public body. **Privatized** = was state-owned, later sold. **Private concession** = private consortium running a state-granted concession. **Private landholders** = private farms, ranches or land grabbers.

## 1. Rio Grande do Sul floods 2024 and the Porto Alegre flood-defence failure (DMAE)

- **Date:** May 2024.
- **Place:** Rio Grande do Sul state; Porto Alegre central neighborhoods.
- **Responsible party and ownership:** Porto Alegre's DMAE (municipal water and drainage utility, **State/municipal**). Pumping stations and gates were run by DMAE. Not privatized.
- **Deaths:** The state Defesa Civil count rose from 177 (June 2024) to 182 (July 2024) and 184–185 in later updates. Sources: [IstoÉ Dinheiro](https://istoedinheiro.com.br/mortes-por-enchentes-do-rio-grande-do-sul-chegam-a-177), [Revista Oeste](https://www.revistaoeste.com/brasil/rio-grande-do-sul-confirma-184-vitima-das-enchentes-de-2024/), [SBT News](https://sbtnews.sbt.com.br/noticia/brasil/rio-grande-do-sul-mortes-nas-enchentes-de-2024-chegam-a-185). My search did not return an exact "183" figure.
- **Affected:** City figures cited in a Sul21 piece: 30% of the municipal territory affected, 160,000 people, 93,000 homes, 31,000 rescued. Source: [Sul21](https://sul21.com.br/?p=263760) (figures attributed to the city, not independently checked).
- **Environmental and infrastructure damage:** Gates left without maintenance leaked; pumping stations stopped, so central water could not drain. Sources: [Carta Capital](https://www.cartacapital.com.br/?p=428724), [Sul21](https://sul21.com.br/?p=263760). A former DMAE/DEP director said he would not have opened the gates: [IHU/Unisinos](https://ihu.unisinos.br/639719-eu-nao-teria-aberto-as-comportas-diz-ex-diretor-do-dep-e-do-dmae).
- **Warnings:** The system had already failed in the 2023 flood. A repair request estimated at R$ 60,000 was reportedly disputed by DMAE. Source: [Sul21](https://sul21.com.br/?p=263760) (one-source claim, contested).
- **Legal outcome:** Not found in my search. The mayor denied the maintenance accusation. Later, R$ 11 million was announced for gate works (2025): [Prefeitura de Porto Alegre](https://prefeitura.poa.br/sites/default/files/usu_doc/noticias/2025/05/02/Porto%20Alegre%20Forte.pdf).
- **Caveat:** Several sources are partisan. Maintenance versus design failure is disputed.

## 2. Madeira river flood 2014 (Jirau and Santo Antônio dams, Porto Velho)

- **Date:** February–March 2014.
- **Place:** Porto Velho and Jaci-Paraná, Rondônia; upstream to Acre and Bolivia.
- **Responsible parties and ownership:** Jirau (ESBR consortium) and Santo Antônio (Madeira Energia consortium). **Private concessions** granted by federal auction in 2008 (auction date from my background knowledge, not verified in my sources). Consortium members in the Jirau case included Chesf and Eletrosul (state, Eletrobras group), so the consortium is mixed.
- **Scale:** Porto Velho reached 19.74 m on 30 March 2014. The flood left 4,937 families homeless in the municipality. Source: [Terra Plural (UEPG)](https://revistas.uepg.br/index.php/tp/article/download/9714/5612/34454). Fearnside gives a peak flow of 60,066 m³/s: [Fearnside 2015](https://antigo-philip.inpa.gov.br/publ_livres/2015/Rio_Madeira_Espada_de_Damocles-2-Risco_para_Porto_Velho.pdf).
- **Deaths:** Not found in my search.
- **Operational dispute:** Santo Antônio shut down 11 turbines during the flood. Jirau blamed Santo Antônio's operation for part of the damage in Jaci-Paraná and Porto Velho. The Sipam blamed rainfall and the Abunã river. Sources: [IHU](https://ihu.unisinos.br/noticias/528747-moradores-relacionam-hidreletricas-a-cheia-historica-do-rio-madeira), [IHU](https://ihu.unisinos.br/noticias/528788-usinas-trocam-acusacoes-por-cheia-no-rio-madeira), [Notícias Agrícolas](https://www.noticiasagricolas.com.br/noticia/136065).
- **Warnings:** UNIR researcher Artur Moret warned of backwater effects that could reach Rondônia and Bolivia: [IHU](https://ihu.unisinos.br/noticias/529173-hidreletricas-do-madeira-usinas-podem-resultar-em-catastrofe-alerta-pesquisador).
- **Legal outcome:** In March 2014 a federal court ordered the companies to redo the environmental studies: [Conjur](https://www.conjur.com.br/?p=487463).
- **Environmental damage:** Backwater and flood changes; the full environmental accounting is not in my sources.

## 3. Petrópolis landslides and floods, February 2022

- **Date:** 15–16 February 2022.
- **Place:** Petrópolis, Rio de Janeiro state.
- **Responsible party:** Municipal and state land-use and housing policy. Hillside occupation was the main driver. No single operator.
- **Deaths:** Most sources give 231–233. Defesa Civil Nacional gives at least 231. Source: [Correio Braziliense](https://www.correiobraziliense.com.br/brasil/2022/03/4989369-mortos-em-petropolis-chegam-a-231-na-tragedia-mais-fatal-da-historia-da-cidade.html). The count was lower in early reports.
- **Displaced:** Niterói civil defence report cites 876 displaced and 5 missing (within the Rio state report set; figure given as-is).
- **Risk background:** A 2017 municipal risk plan found nearly 20% of the territory at high or very high risk. Source: [Petrópolis risk study (summary in search)](https://bdta.abcd.usp.br/bitstreams/78c82c31-f2c8-4ba6-baa0-2a9aeb5add45). A USP thesis reports 86% of recorded occurrences were landslides.
- **Legal outcome:** Not found in my search.
- **Technical report:** CPRM post-disaster report: [CPRM](https://rigeo.cprm.gov.br/bitstream/doc/22668/1/Relatorio%20Tecnico%20Petropolis.pdf).

**Sources:**

## 4. Pantanal fires 2020

- **Date:** 2020 dry season (peak in Sept–Oct).
- **Place:** Pantanal, Mato Grosso and Mato Grosso do Sul.
- **Responsible party:** Mostly **private landholders** (per the Ministério Público report). Authorities did not establish a single cause.
- **Burned area (sources disagree):** About 4.5 million ha Jan–Nov per MP-MS/MT. About 30% of the Pantanal (about 3.9 million ha per one analysis). Satellite products range from 14,307 km² to 44,998 km² depending on method. Sources: [Poder360](https://www.poder360.com.br/meio-ambiente/em-2020-grandes-incendios-queimaram-30-da-area-do-pantanal/), [FAPESP](https://agencia.fapesp.br/em-2020-grandes-incendios-queimaram-30-da-area-do-pantanal/44693), [MP-MS/MT report](https://ecoa.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Relatório-Geral-MP-MS_MT_Queima-Pantanal-2020.pdf).
- **Causes:** Extreme drought (FAPESP) and human ignition (MP report: most fire points were inside private properties, far from roads and rivers).
- **Deaths:** Not found.
- **Legal outcome:** Not found in my search.

**Sources:**

## 5. "Dia do Fogo" (Day of Fire), Pará, August 2019

- **Date:** 10–11 August 2019.
- **Place:** Novo Progresso and the southwest of Pará, Amazon.
- **Responsible party:** **Private landholders** and local business people, organized by WhatsApp (Agência Pública; Greenpeace).
- **Hotspots:** 101 on 9 August, 715 on 10 August (per Greenpeace and Pública). Source: [Greenpeace](https://www.greenpeace.org/brasil/florestas/dia-do-fogo-completa-um-ano-com-legado-de-impunidade/).
- **Deaths:** None listed in my sources.
- **Warnings:** An MPF-PA notice to Ibama on 7 August warned of the action. Source: [Poder360](https://www.poder360.com.br/brasil/forca-nacional-ignorou-alerta-sobre-dia-do-fogo-mostram-documentos/).
- **Legal outcome:** Federal Police opened an inquiry on 26 August 2019. The inquiries were archived in the first half of 2024 without charges. Source: [IHU](https://ihu.unisinos.br/643021-investigacoes-sobre-dia-do-fogo-sao-arquivadas-ninguem-e-responsabilizado), [Agência Pública](https://apublica.org/nota/investigacoes-sobre-dia-do-fogo-na-amazonia-nao-identificaram-culpados/). Sources differ on whether the coordination was proven. The Civil Police of Pará found no sign of coordinated action in a preliminary inquiry.

## 6. Pau D'Arco massacre (Santa Lúcia farm), Pará, May 2017

- **Date:** 24 May 2017.
- **Place:** Fazenda Santa Lúcia, Pau D'Arco, Pará.
- **Responsible party:** **State police** (civil and military police, Pará state government). The land was **private** (occupied by landless workers).
- **Deaths:** 10 landless rural workers killed in the operation (Bahia Notícias; Alma Preta reports the same count).
- **Legal outcome:** Eleven military police and two civil police had temporary arrest warrants granted. Arrests followed in Dec 2017 (STJ order). Later, the TJ-PA released all military police involved (Dec 2017). Per Alma Preta (2022), of 155 police involved only two were convicted. That figure may be out of date. Sources: [Brasil de Fato](https://www.brasildefato.com.br/2017/12/19/com-policiais-soltos-sobreviventes-do-massacre-de-pau-darco-temem-represalias/), [JB](https://www.jb.com.br/pais/noticias/2017/12/22/amp/stj-determina-a-prisao-de-oito-policiais-envolvidos-na-chacina-de-pau-d-arco.html), [Alma Preta](https://almapreta.com.br/?p=4135).
- **Other:** The alleged masterminds were still under investigation as of 2022 per Alma Preta.

## 7. Chapada do Apodi aerial spraying and water contamination, Ceará and Rio Grande do Norte

- **Date:** Monitoring since 2006; state ban passed 2018, sanctioned January 2019.
- **Place:** Chapada do Apodi, between Limoeiro do Norte and Quixeré (Ceará) and nearby RN.
- **Responsible party:** **Private agribusiness** (export fruit and melon monoculture; aircraft and tractors spraying).
- **Health findings (UFC Tramas study):** 97% of workers exposed; about 60% reported acute poisoning. All 46 drinking-water samples from communities contained pesticides. Source: [UFC study PDF](https://prppg.ufc.br/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/uolagrotoxicos.pdf).
- **Displacement or affected:** Drift reached homes.
- **Deaths:** Community leader Zé Maria was killed; his killing was reported as retaliation for his opposition to aerial spraying (source: [IHU](https://ihu.unisinos.br/noticias/523551-monocultivo-e-veneno-ameacam-chapada-do-apodi-rn) and UFC summary). Not confirmed as a court finding.
- **Legal outcome:** Limoeiro do Norte repealed its municipal ban a week after passage. Ceará's state ban was sanctioned in January 2019. Constitutionality was challenged at the STF (ADI 6137). Outcome not found. Sources: [Brasil de Fato](https://www.brasildefato.com.br/2019/01/10/governador-do-ceara-sanciona-lei-que-proibe-pulverizacao-aerea-nas-lavouras/), [Brasil de Fato](https://www.brasildefato.com.br/2018/12/19/assembleia-legislativa-proibe-pulverizacao-aerea-de-agrotoxicos-no-ceara-entenda/).

## 8. Rio Verde (GO) school pesticide spraying, May 2013

- **Date:** 3 May 2013.
- **Place:** São José do Pontal rural school, Rio Verde, Goiás. (Not Primavera do Leste. The brief named Primavera do Leste, but my searches found no 2013 school incident there. Primavera do Leste's documented case is a 2017 student poisoning per Human Rights Watch.)
- **Responsible party:** Aviation contractor **Aerotex** spraying **Engeo Pleno** (Syngenta), a **private** company. Municipality said the release over the school was a mistake.
- **Victims (sources disagree):** 36 children + 2 teachers (one source); 29 children + 8 adults (another); about 92 students and teachers (a third). Source: [Brasil de Fato](https://www.brasildefato.com.br/2018/06/20/pulverizacao-de-agrotoxicos-sobre-escola-segue-impune-ha-5-anos) and [Senado](https://www12.senado.leg.br/noticias/materias/2013/09/19/vitimas-de-intoxicacao-por-agrotoxico-denunciam-falta-de-assistencia).
- **Deaths:** None found.
- **Legal outcome:** The pilot was arrested and bailed out (JB, May 2013): [JB](https://www.jb.com.br/pais/noticias/2013/05/05/amp/go-piloto-que-pulverizou-agrotoxico-em-escola-e-solto-apos-pagar-fianca.html). A 2018 report said the case was still unpunished. The MPF later opened a new investigation into irregular spraying in Rio Verde: [Jornal Opção](https://www.jornalopcao.com.br/ultimas-noticias/mpf-cobra-da-prefeitura-de-rio-verde-assistencia-a-atingidos-por-aplicacao-irregular-de-agrotoxicos-203711/).

## 9. Região Serrana landslides and floods, Rio de Janeiro state, January 2011

- **Date:** 11–12 January 2011.
- **Place:** Teresópolis, Nova Friburgo, Petrópolis, and others.
- **Responsible party:** Hillside occupation and weak land-use and prevention policy. Public bodies (municipal and state) are the responsible parties. No single operator.
- **Deaths (sources disagree):** 287 on 13 January (state count); 717 by 19 January (Polícia Civil); about 900 (academic 2012: 905; a 2022 report: 918). Nova Friburgo alone: 420–426. Sources: [Exame](https://exame.com/brasil/governo-do-rio-ja-confirma-287-mortes-na-regiao-serrana/), [JB](https://www.jb.com.br/tragedia-na-serra/noticias/2011/01/19/amp/ja-sao-717-os-mortos-na-regiao-serrana-do-rio.html).
- **Affected:** Not found.
- **Warnings and follow-up:** Folha PE reported that after the 2011 rains, municipalities used only about 50% of the funds for prevention: [Folha PE](https://www.folhape.com.br/noticia/amp/216026/apos-chuvas-de-2011-prefeituras-da-regiao-serrana-usaram-so-50-dos/). Confea reported more than 700 hillsides still at risk: [Confea](https://www.confea.org.br/node/111265).
- **Legal outcome:** Not found in my search.

- **Date:** March 2011 (a second strike followed in March 2012, about 20,000 workers).

- **Responsible party:** **Private concession**: ESBR consortium (GDF Suez, Camargo Corrêa, with Chesf and Eletrosul from the state group).

## 11. Belo Monte (Volta Grande do Xingu and Altamira), 2011 to present

- **Date:** Concession auction April 2010; contract signed August 2010; construction from 2011.
- **Place:** Altamira, Pará; Volta Grande do Xingu (diverted river).
- **Responsible party and ownership:** **Private concession** to **Norte Energia S.A.** The consortium was led by Chesf (state group, under Eletrobras) with 49.98%, plus private partners (Contern, Cetenco, Gaia Energia, and others). Source: [KHL](https://www.khl.com/news/Belo-Monte-concession-contract-signed/1057938.article), [Bahia Notícias](https://www.bahianoticias.com.br/noticia/191579-tcu-investiga-possibilidade-de-conluio-em-licitacao-de-belo-monte). The TCU investigated possible collusion at the 2010 auction; Eletrobras subsidiaries Furnas and Eletrosul were in the rival group.
- **Displacement:** EIA projected 20–30% of Altamira's 100,000 people displaced permanently. An independent assessment found the real number directly affected could be double the official figure. Source: [WRM](https://www.wrm.org.uy/print/pdf/node/10696/pt).
- **Social collapse:** MPF 2019 inspection found "desestruturação da vida econômica e social" in Volta Grande, with 74 findings and signs of quiet expulsion. Source: [WRM](https://www.wrm.org.uy/print/pdf/node/10696/pt) (MPF summary as cited).
- **Water and humanitarian emergency:** MPF lawsuit says over 600 families face a humanitarian emergency (water, sanitation, communication). IBAMA was included for failing to supervise. Source: [MPF-PA](https://www.mpf.mp.br/o-mpf/unidades/pr-pa/noticias/mpf-reforca-pedidos-para-garantir-agua-saneamento-e-comunicacao-a-familias-atingidas-por-belo-monte-no-para).
- **Human-rights council:** In 2016 the national human-rights council warned of a foreseeable disaster and illegal removal of residents. Norte Energia denied it. Source: [IHU](https://ihu.unisinos.br/561192-desastre-em-belo-monte-e-iminente-diz-conselho-de-direitos-humanos).
- **Deaths:** None found.
- **Environmental damage:** Irregular flow in the Volta Grande disrupts aquatic fauna and riverine peoples (MPF).
- **Legal outcome:** Ongoing litigation. The latest status was not found in my sources.

## 12. Algodões I dam failure, Piauí, May 2009

- **Date:** 27 May 2009.
- **Place:** Cocal and Buriti dos Lopes, Piauí.

## 14. Endosulfan in breast milk, Lucas do Rio Verde (chlordecone-type case in Brazil)

- **Date of study:** Samples collected Feb–June 2010 (mothers who gave birth 2007–2010). Study by Wanderlei Pignati (UFMT).
- **Place:** Lucas do Rio Verde, Mato Grosso.

A 2014 article says endosulfan is now banned along with DDT (banned 2009). I did not find an exact 2013 act.
- **Deaths:** Not applicable.
- **Legal outcome:** Not found beyond the regulatory steps above.
- **Note for the brief:** No Brazilian chlordecone-equivalent was found. Endosulfan is the closest documented case of persistent organochlorine contamination in a rural population.

## Patterns

1. **Ownership and concession.** Hydro disasters with the most damage were state-run (Balbina, Tucuruí, Sobradinho, Itaipu, Camará, Algodões) or private concessions with state partners (Belo Monte, Jirau, Santo Antônio). The state's privatization of Eletrobras (law July 2021; subsidiaries Chesf, Furnas, Eletronorte and Eletrosul still under its group) means that the same state-linked owners now sit inside a privatized parent. Source: [Correio Braziliense](https://www.correiobraziliense.com.br/economia/2021/07/amp/4937267-bolsonaro-sanciona-com-vetos-lei-de-privatizacao-da-eletrobras.html).

2. **Warnings ignored and regulator capacity.** Gates unmaintained (Porto Alegre 2024), dams without maintenance (Camará, Algodões), and warnings from MPF and researchers before the 2014 Madeira flood and the 2011 Serrana landslides. The state, not the operator, usually paid for prevention failures.

3. **Who paid and who lost.** Displaced riverine, indigenous and landless populations bore most costs. Compensation was late, partial or disputed (Tucuruí, Balbina, Sobradinho). Land and violence cases (Eldorado, Corumbiara, Pau d'Arco, Chico Mendes) show near-total impunity for the landholders or commanders, except Eldorado (Pantoja and a major convicted, served late). Pesticide and fire cases (Dia do Fogo, Rio Verde 2013, Chapada do Apodi) show very few convictions.
