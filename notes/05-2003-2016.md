# Era 2003-2016 (Lula 1-2, Dilma 1-2): public power, land and assets handed to private interests

Method note: figures come from web searches of Portuguese and English press, government and TCU material. Where a figure comes from a secondary summary of a primary document (TCU, Science paper), it is flagged "to verify in primary". Items marked UNVERIFIED are from my background knowledge, not a source, and must be checked before use. Oil concession rounds (ANP rounds 5-12) were NOT researched (see gaps).

## 1. BNDES "national champion" equity in JBS (and Bertin)
- **Years:** 2007-2013 (TCU audit 2015-2018). **Government:** Lula 2 and Dilma 1.
- **Transferred:** BNDESPar equity and debenture injections. PF/TCU table sums R$ 8,110,371,712.96 across the JBS group (Acordao 3011/2015-TCU-Plenario). BNDESPar went to 12.95% of JBS after the Swift deal, 19.42% after National Beef, 33.40% after Pilgrim's debenture conversion. R$ 2.5 bn into Bertin in 2008; Swift: US$ 750 m (R$ 2.3 bn in current values). Source: https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/agronegocio/pecuaria/bndes-perdeu-mais-de-r-1-bilhao-em-compra-de-frigorifico-1ajtajdsf3p6fubear6qka232 ; Senate table via https://legis.senado.leg.br/sdleg-getter/documento/download/8359a698-18c3-4ac6-9b34-b147b77b1126 (search-summary; verify).
- **Who got it:** JBS/Friboi (Batista family), to buy foreign meatpackers.
- **Price/terms vs value (disputed):** TCU found loss of R$ 670 m historical (R$ 1.17 bn updated) on Bertin; a further R$ 347.7 m because BNDES did not charge a 10% fee on a conversion; press summary "almost R$ 1 bn loss" (https://www.jornalopcao.com.br/bastidores/jbs-deu-prejuizo-de-quase-1-bilhao-de-reais-ao-bndes-revela-o-tcu-94710/). Other press reports cite R$ 711 m (https://www.congressoemfoco.com.br/noticia/40399/bndes-perdeu-r-711-milhoes-com-a-jbs-diz-tcu), R$ 950 m on Pilgrim's (https://www.poder360.com.br/economia/tcu-aponta-prejuizo-de-r-950-mi-do-bndes-em-operacao-com-a-jbs) and R$ 5.08 bn in a 2018 audit (https://oantagonista.com.br/economia/bndes-perdeu-mais-de-r-5-bi-na-jbs-diz-o-tcu/). **Other side:** BNDES says the operations gave a R$ 2.8 bn positive result; a later TCU special-accounts decision judged the responsible officials' accounts regular (https://portal.tcu.gov.br/imprensa/noticias/compra-de-acoes-da-empresa-jbs-pela-bndes-participacoes-nao-gerou-debitos.htm). Figures conflict; no single reconciled number.
- **Legal mechanism:** BNDESPar discretionary equity purchases under the "national champions" industrial policy.
- **Costs borne by:** Treasury/BNDES (which is funded by Treasury loans, see case 17).
- **After:** 2017 JBS plea deal (Joesley Batista) and PF/TCU probes; JBS became world's largest meat processor.
- **Sources:** as above; https://www.tribunapr.com.br/noticias/economia/relatorio-do-tcu-indica-favorecimento-do-bndes-a-jbs/

## 2. Oi / Brasil Telecom (BrT) merger, "supertele"
- **Years:** 2008-2009 (collapse 2016). **Government:** Lula 2.
- **Transferred:** state-sanctioned regulatory change plus subsidized credit. BNDES lent R$ 2.569 bn for restructuring Telemar Participacoes' control (https://exame.com/negocios/bndes-considera-positiva-possivel-alianca-oi-portugal-telecom-582732/ ); BNDES owned 16.89% of Telemar holding (same). Banco do Brasil financed R$ 4.3 bn for the BrT purchase; private banks R$ 5.6 bn in promissory notes (https://www.folhavitoria.com.br/economia/campea-nacional-oi-sucumbiu-a-crise/ , search summary). Earlier, Oi got R$ 6.5 bn and BrT R$ 6.1 bn from BNDES in 1998-2007 (same). A BNDES loan of R$ 4.4 bn to Oi was approved Nov 2009 (https://teletime.com.br/18/11/2009/bndes-aprova-emprestimo-de-r-44-bilhoes-para-oi/ ).
- **Legal mechanism:** Decree 6.654 of 20 Nov 2008 (new Plano Geral de Outorgas) https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2007-2010/2008/decreto/D6654.htm ; press reports Lula authorised a legal change to permit the buy (Folha Vitoria link above). Contested: a law firm (BKBG) argued the PGO need not change (https://www.baguete.com.br/public/noticias/fusao-oibrt-pgo-nao-precisaria-ser-alterado). I did not confirm exactly which act removed the cross-ownership barrier.
- **Who got it:** Telemar/Oi controllers (Andrade Gutierrez, La Fonte/Jereissati, funds), then Portugal Telecom in 2010-2014.
- **Costs borne:** BNDES and public banks as creditors; consumers (service quality; not sourced).
- **After:** Oi filed judicial recovery on 20 June 2016 with R$ 65 bn in debt, then largest in Brazil (https://itforum.com.br/?p=115936 ). BNDES had suspended payments 180 days beforehand (https://teletime.com.br/22/06/2016/oi-confirma-suspensao-de-pagamentos-de-r-8167-milhoes-bndes-e-banco-do-brasil/ ). Plan approved 2018; recovery closed Dec 2022 (https://portaltj.tjrj.jus.br/noticias/noticia/-/visualizar-conteudo/5111210/132533679 ).

## 3. Eike Batista's EBX group (OGX, MMX, LLX/Prumo, OSX, MPX)
- **Years:** 2007-2014 (collapse 2013). **Government:** Lula 2, Dilma 1.
- **Transferred:** BNDES/FMM loans and equity. Totals disputed: Exame says about R$ 10.4 bn since 2007 (https://exame.com/negocios/noticias/25-revelacoes-sobre-a-crise-de-eike-batista); Eike told the CPI about R$ 10 bn for MPX, LLX, EMX; BNDES president Coutinho told the Senate effectively contracted loans were about R$ 6 bn (https://www.congressoemfoco.com.br/noticia/52451/eike-diz-que-recursos-do-bndes-foram-apenas-parte-dos-seus-investimentos). Items: R$ 1.3 bn to MMX for Acu port (Jan 2010); R$ 600 m MPX debentures (2011); R$ 2.7 bn FMM-sourced for OSX shipyard (https://www.infomoney.com.br/?p=557125 ); R$ 1.344 bn approved June 2012 for the shipyard (same search set). Part of Eike's personal assets pledged to R$ 2.3 bn of BNDES loans (Bloomberg via Exame).
- **Who got it:** Eike Batista companies.
- **Terms vs value:** Eike told the CPI BNDES had "zero loss" (https://www.congressoemfoco.com.br/noticia/52450/bndes-nao-teve-prejuizo-com-investimentos-no-grupo-ebx-afirma-eike); no independent loss estimate found.
- **Legal mechanism:** BNDES/BNDESPar credit and FMM (Fundo da Marinha Mercante) financing.
- **After:** OGX filed the largest judicial recovery in Latin America on 30 Oct 2013 (R$ 11.2 bn owed to main creditors per Epoca) (https://www.jb.com.br/economia/noticias/2013/10/30/amp/ogx-entra-com-pedido-de-recuperacao-judicial.html ; https://blog.grupogen.com.br/juridico/postagens/noticias/professor-cassio-cavalli-autor-da-editora-forense-fala-sobre-recuperacao-judicial-da-ogx/ ); OSX also filed (https://exame.com/negocios/justica-acata-pedido-de-recuperacao-judicial-da-osx-2 ); OGX recovery closed Aug 2017 (https://www.moneytimes.com.br/ogx-de-eike-encerra-recuperacao-judicial/ ).

- **Transferred:** BNDES to put in up to R$ 2.4 bn in the new VCP to enable the Aracruz acquisition (https://www.en.investe.sp.gov.br/news/post/the-aracruz-vcp-merger). VCP agreed to pay R$ 2.7 bn for the Lorentzen family's 28% of voting shares. Currency of loss unresolved by my sources.
- **Who got it:** Votorantim group; Fibria ended with BNDESPar 34.9% and Votorantim 29.3% (investe.sp link). BNDES conditioned support on a prior deal on derivative losses with creditor banks (same).
- **After:** Fibria later merged with Suzano (https://moneytimes.com.br/suzano-e-fibria-anunciam-fusao/ ). Outcome for BNDES not quantified here (gap).

## 5. Belo Monte dam (Xingu, PA)
- **Years:** auction April 2010; construction 2011-2016+; **Government:** Lula 2, Dilma 1.
- **Transferred:** the concession to Norte Energia (led by Chesf/Eletrobras; Eletronorte 19.98%, Eletrobras 15%, Chesf 15%, pension funds Petros and Funcef 10% each per Agencia Publica, https://apublica.org/?p=34459 ). Bid R$ 77.97/MWh versus ceiling R$ 83 (6.02% discount) (https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/economia/chesf-e-queiroz-galvao-vencem-leilao-de-belo-monte-0knsdijxm7roqbti5z1nxaqtq). BNDES approved R$ 22.5 bn in Nov 2012 over 30 years (https://ihu.unisinos.br/noticias/515876-belo-monte-tera-r-225-bi-do-bndes), the biggest in its history per MPF (a request of R$ 24.5 bn was cited).
- **Value vs price:** Cost estimates: R$ 19 bn at the auction (https://apet.org.br/?p=87150), R$ 25 bn by 2012, MPF used about R$ 30 bn, later reports about R$ 31 bn (outraspalavras guide https://outraspalavras.net/outrasmidias/guia-belo-monte/). Rogerio Werneck (Estadao) argued true cost-reflective tariff was at least R$ 100/MWh (via Gazeta/acendebrasil link). MPF said BNDES showed no evidence of viability/risk analysis (outraspalavras).
- **Legal mechanism:** Legislative decree authorising works without prior Indigenous consultation; ANEEL auction; BNDES credit. MPF suit (filed 2006) lost 2-1 at TRF-1 in 2011; judge Selene Almeida voted to annul until Indigenous were heard; ILO Convention 169 ratified 2003 (https://site-antigo.socioambiental.org/en/print/2079 ; https://www.sedep.com.br/?p=53659). Final merits outcome not confirmed.
- **Costs borne:** Indigenous and riverine people (Cimi estimated at least 30 peoples affected; government says no direct impact on Indigenous lands, outraspalavras guide); treasury via BNDES and state-owned shareholders; environment.
- **After (corruption):** Lava Jato plea deals: Camargo Correa execs said 1% of the contract went to PT and PMDB politicians; Andrade Gutierrez deal cites about R$ 150 m in bribes; R$ 15 m to Delfim Netto alleged (https://www.tribunapr.com.br/noticias/politica/delacao-de-andrade-gutierrez-confirma-r-150-milhoes-de-propina-em-belo-monte/ ; https://congressoemfoco.com.br/noticia/28293/lava-jato-acusa-delfim-netto-de-receber-r-15-milhoes-em-propina-de-consorcio-para-construcao-de-belo-monte). Allegations denied by MDB. TCU opened an inquiry June 2015; result not found. Contractors asked for another R$ 1 bn (https://www.ihu.unisinos.br/noticias/520145-construtora-pede-mais-r-1-bi-por-belo-monte).

## 7. Cessao onerosa and the 2010 Petrobras capitalisation
- **Years:** 2010. **Government:** Lula 2.
- **Transferred:** Lei 12.276/2010 gave Petrobras rights to produce up to 5 bn boe in six Santos Basin pre-salt areas without a bid. Price average US$ 8.51/bbl, US$ 42.5 bn = R$ 74.8 bn, paid in Treasury securities within the capitalisation (https://www.gov.br/anp/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/apresentacoes-palestras/2019/arquivos/apresentacao-rodada-cessao-onerosa.pdf ; https://eixos.com.br/cessao-onerosa-o-que-esta-em-jogo/ ). Small variations by source (US$ 8.56; R$ 75 bn).
- **Who got it:** Petrobras (state-controlled, with private minority shareholders) in exchange for fresh capital; the state boosted its stake.
- **Terms vs value:** Petrobras later argued it overpaid since oil fell and the fields held a surplus, estimated 9.8-15.2 bn bbl in total, ANP later lower; sought compensation; surplus volumes sold in 2019 round (same sources; https://braziljournal.com/show-me-the-money-os-riscos-no-leilao-da-cessao-onerosa).
- **Note for pattern:** here the transfer goes state->state-owned firm, not purely private; critics (e.g. https://monitormercantil.com.br/denunciando-a-entrega-da-cess-o-onerosa/) call it a giveaway. Include only as context.

## 8. Pre-salt regime (Lei 12.351/2010) and the Libra auction (2013)
- **Years:** 2010-2013. **Government:** Lula 2, Dilma 1.
- **Mechanism:** production sharing instead of concession for pre-salt; Petrobras sole operator with minimum 30%; Social Fund created (art. 47) (https://planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_Ato2007-2010/2010/Lei/L12351.htm). Lei 12.734/2012 redistributed royalties.
- **Libra (21 Oct 2013):** one bid only, Petrobras 40% (operator), Shell 20%, Total 20%, CNPC 10%, CNOOC 10%; profit oil to Union 41.65% (the minimum in the notice); signing bonus R$ 15 bn paid 27 Nov 2013 (https://www.cms-lawnow.com/ealerts/2013/10/brazil-results-of-the-1st-presalt-licensing-round ; https://exame.com/economia/noticias/consorcio-ofereceu-minimo-exigido-pelo-edital-de-libra ; https://exame.com/economia/empresas-deposita-r-15-bi-do-bonus-de-libra/). Critics: no competition; government used the bonus to balance 2013 accounts (same sources). Part of the area later returned (2021).
- **Later (context):** Lei 13.365/2016 ended mandatory Petrobras operatorship (from my background knowledge and the Senate bill reference: https://static.poder360.com.br/2025/06/DOC-Avulso-inicial-da-materia-20190528.pdf ; UNVERIFIED final law number).
- **Ambiguity:** this case is a state retaining control (not strictly a handover). Note as counter-example.

## 9. Airport concessions: Guarulhos, Viracopos, Brasilia (2012) and Galeao, Confins (2013-14)
- **Years:** auction 6 Feb 2012. **Government:** Dilma 1.
- **Transferred:** 20-30 year concessions. Total offered R$ 24.535 bn (https://repositorio.itl.org.br/jspui/bitstream/123456789/269/1/Economia%20em%20Foco_novembro_2013%20-%20Concess%c3%a3o%20de%20aeroportos.pdf ). Guarulhos (Invepar/ACSA): R$ 16.213 bn, premium 373% over minimum R$ 3.424 bn, 20 yrs; Brasilia (Inframerica): R$ 4.5 bn, premium 673% over R$ 582 m; Viracopos (Aeroportos Brasil: TPI/UTC/Egis): R$ 3.82 bn, premium ~160%, 30 yrs (https://www.baguete.com.br/noticias/invepar-triunfo-e-engevix-levam-aeroportos ; http://memoria.ebc.com.br/agenciabrasil/new-in-english/2012-02-07/news-english-%E2%80%93-airport-privatization-auction-considered-success-bids-were-five-times-what-gove ). Infraero kept 49% of each concessionaire (ITL).
- **Terms vs value:** large premiums were presented as success; critics later saw over-bidding that led to distress.
- **After:** Viracopos: concessionaire filed for judicial recovery May 2018 (debt R$ 2.88 bn; about R$ 2.6-2.7 bn owed, mostly BNDES), shareholders asked to return the concession in July 2017, relicitation (first in Brazilian airport history), plan approved Feb 2020 (https://www.agazeta.com.br/economia/aeroporto-de-viracopos-sera-devolvido-pela-concessionaria-e-relicitado-0220 ; https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/infra/anac-e-viracopos-vivem-historico-de-conflitos-e-acoes-judiciais-desde-2014/). Odebrecht/UTC shareholders implicated in Lava Jato (background; UNVERIFIED here). Galeao/Confins: not researched (gap; Galeao concessionaire later returned the concession, UNVERIFIED).

## 11. Petrobras contractor cartel and kickbacks (Lava Jato)
- **Years:** about 2004-2014 (revealed 2014). **Government:** Lula 1-2, Dilma 1.
- **Transferred:** inflated contracts. Ex-director Paulo Roberto Costa said cartel worked inside and outside Petrobras; PP, PT and PMDB divided directorships to collect 1-3% on big contracts (https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/vida-publica/cartel-de-empreiteiras-atuou-em-refinaria-do-pr-diz-costa-eet1tbuvl6d58uwdegdddx74e ; https://www.conjur.com.br/2015-fev-20/cinco-acoes-mpf-cobra-447-bilhoes-desvios-petrobras -- MPF suits claim R$ 44.7 bn, per title; verify). Odebrecht and Andrade Gutierrez led the cartel per PF/MPF (https://www.congressoemfoco.com.br/noticia/74961/odebrecht-e-andrade-gutierrez-lideravam-cartel-dizem-pf-e-mpf).
- **Size:** Petrobras recognised R$ 6.19 bn loss from corruption in the 2014 balance sheet, using 3% of contract values as a method (https://www.infomoney.com.br/?p=363746 ; https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/economia/petrobras-desvaloriza-r-14-bilhoes-apos-balanco-desconsiderar-corrupcao-ejbl0c8fcxeag2wqdsn9xi91q). PF estimated losses up to 20% of contract values while plea-bargainers admitted 3% (STJ clipping https://scon.stj.jus.br/docs_intranet/noticias/Newsletter/2015/np0410.pdf ). Costa said 3% was embedded in contractor margins and denied overpricing at some works (conflict). Youssef said 60% of the bribe fed political agents. 2014 net loss R$ 21.58 bn (asset impairments R$ 44.63 bn, a separate item).
- **Costs borne:** Petrobras (minority shareholders, Treasury), taxpayers; workers lost jobs after (not sourced).
- **After:** plea deals, leniency agreements (amounts not researched), contractors' decline.

## 12. MP 579/2012 (Lei 12.783/2013): electricity concession renewals
- **Years:** Sept 2012-2013. **Government:** Dilma 1.
- **Granted/transferred:** early renewal of generation and transmission concessions for up to 30 years at lower revenue; indemnity for unamortised assets; sector charges cut (RGR to end); promised bill reduction about 16-20% (sources disagree) (https://www.camara.leg.br/noticias/384998-renovacao-das-concessoes-do-setor-eletrico-gera-polemica-no-congresso ; https://www12.senado.leg.br/publicacoes/estudos-legislativos/tipos-de-estudos/sumarios-de-proposicoes/mpv579).
- **Size:** MME expected indemnities of about R$ 20 bn; Eletrobras alone expected about R$ 30 bn; later corrections added about R$ 10 bn per rapporteur Eduardo Cunha (https://www.infomoney.com.br/?p=593603 , via search summary).
- **Who got it/cost:** this is a reverse case (state forced concessionaires to cut tariffs; companies like Cesp, Cemig, Copel resisted). The cost fell on the Treasury (CDE transfers) and Eletrobras; I did not confirm the Treasury amount. Eletrobras share price collapsed (not sourced). Include as counter-pattern: tariff cut financed by public money, benefiting consumers and electro-intensive firms.

## 14. Terra Legal (MP 458/2009 -> Lei 11.952/2009): land regularisation in the Amazon
- **Years:** 2009+. **Government:** Lula 2.
- **Transferred:** federal public land to occupants: up to 15 fiscal modules, max 1,500 ha, onerous title price below market for larger areas (the below-market price detail is from background; UNVERIFIED). CPT called it a legalisation of land grabbing (https://www.congressoemfoco.com.br/noticia/78037/pastoral-da-terra-publica-nota-de-repudio-a-mp-da-amazonia ). Occupation cut-off date 2004 (UNVERIFIED).
- **Size:** TCU: 28,331 titles to 31 Dec 2017 covering 2,057,119.95 ha (via https://static.congressoemfoco.com.br/2020/04/nota-tecnica-8-2020-regularizacao-fundiaria.pdf ); other source says almost 40,000 titles 2012-2017; sources differ on counting.
- **Costs/after:** TCU found poor functioning and incentive to land grabbing; Carta Capital cites TCU that 33% of the area was irregularly taken (date unknown) (https://www.cartacapital.com.br/?p=246086 ). 2015 Chamber committee asked TCU audit (https://www.camara.leg.br/noticias/474929-a-pedido-de-comissao,-tcu-fara-auditoria-no-programa-terra-legal ). Later cap raised toward 2,500 ha under MP 759/2016 (Temer era) (https://apublica.org/2017/07/mais-de-2-mil-imoveis-irregulares-em-terras-publicas-na-amazonia-podem-ser-legalizados-por-mp-da-grilagem/ ). Remote-sensing-only verification was permitted (UNVERIFIED).

## 15. Forest Code 2012 (Lei 12.651): amnesty for past deforestation
- **Years:** 2012 (STF ruling 2018). **Government:** Dilma 1.
- **Granted:** Soares-Filho et al. (Science, 2014) estimate the law amnesties 29 million ha illegally cleared before 2008, keeps 88 million ha legally clearable, and cuts restoration needed from 50 to 21 million ha (-58%) (https://www.ufmg.br/online/arquivos/032981.shtml ; https://ihu.unisinos.br/noticias/530640-codigo-florestal-deve-anistiar-29-milhoes-de-hectares-desmatados). Numbers via press; verify in the Science paper.
- **Who got it:** rural landowners (large and small); legal reserve debts for pre-July-2008 clearing reduced.
- **Disputed:** STF in 2018 held it is not a full amnesty because owners remain subject to penalties if they break regularisation agreements (https://www.brasilagro.com.br/conteudo/em-vitoria-do-agronegocio-stf-mantem-anistia-a-desmatadores.html ; https://www.camara.leg.br/noticias/532920-decisao-do-stf-sobre-codigo-florestal-repercute-entre-partidos). 
- **Costs:** environment, climate, downstream water users.

## 16. PPP law (Lei 11.079/2004) and the first PPP, Metro Line 4 Sao Paulo
- **Years:** law Dec 2004; Line 4 contract 2006. **Government:** Lula 1 (federal), Sao Paulo state (state).
- **Mechanism:** sponsored and administrative concessions; the state guarantees payments. Line 4 (ViaQuatro): public builds tunnels and stations, private buys trains/systems and operates for 30 years (https://www2.senado.leg.br/jspui/bitstream/id/479419/3/complemento_2.htm ; https://legis.senado.leg.br/norma/570528).
- **Critics:** union publication calls it capitalism without risk and a subsidy (advocacy source: https://www.metroviarios.org.br/site/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/especial_contraprivatizacao.pdf ); Sao Paulo legislature document warns of cost to the state (https://www.al.sp.gov.br/spl/2012/12/Propositura/28415101_1113061_pl751-Mensagem%20134.doc). No audited cost figure found (gap). World Cup arena PPPs/BNDES financing not found (gap).

## 17. BNDES funding by the Treasury and PSI subsidised credit ("bolsa-empresario")
- **Years:** 2008-2014. **Government:** Lula 2, Dilma 1.
- **Transferred:** Treasury borrowed at market rates and lent to BNDES at TJLP. Ipea (2010, 2008-2010 data): subsidy R$ 11.6-15.9 bn on R$ 236 bn transferred; Marcio Garcia: Treasury transfers to public banks since 2008 reached R$ 414 bn, nearly 10% of GDP, "bolsa-empresario" cost above 0.6% of GDP (https://www.econ.puc-rio.br/mgarcia/Artigos/Valor%20140523%20MGarcia.pdf ); Folha 2014: R$ 184 bn over four decades; Coutinho (BNDES) argues cost is R$ 44.5 bn with BNDES margin included versus R$ 180.7 bn gross Selic-TJLP (https://pt.org.br/luciano-coutinho-o-bndes-e-o-tesouro-nacional/). Explicit PSI subsidies R$ 8.0 bn (2014), R$ 9.8 bn (2015) (Tesouro bulletin https://www.tesourotransparente.gov.br/ckan/dataset/6d99f766-54ec-4dcf-9376-0b297df3ab17/resource/29e6f62f-d0a8-4879-b8c8-2e3683481ea0/download/Boletim-1o-bimestre-2018--novo-modelo-28-03v16textotabelaIII.pdf). Estimates differ by orders of magnitude depending on method.
- **Who got it:** large firms, via cases 1-4, 18.

## 18. Payroll tax relief (desoneracao da folha)
- **Years:** 2011 pilot, expanded 2012-2015. **Government:** Dilma 1.
- **Size:** cost R$ 3.62 bn (2012, 0.08% GDP) to R$ 25.2 bn (2015, 0.42% GDP); cumulative 2012-2016 R$ 77.9 bn (Receita) / 2012-2017 R$ 91.1 bn (Fazenda); R$ 14.5 bn in 2016 after partial reversal (https://www.baguete.com.br/noticias/desoneracao-da-folha-acabou-para-ti ; https://setcesp.org.br/noticias/governo-eleva-imposto-sobre-folha-de-pagamento-em-5-anos-desoneracoes-somaram-r-78-bilhoes/ ; https://blogdoibre.fgv.br/node/1859).
- **Result:** government studies found small job effects, 30,000-132,634 jobs, hence very high cost per job (https://seer.ufrgs.br/AnaliseEconomica/article/download/71093/52463 ; SPE presentation to Senate).
- **Who bore cost:** social security (Treasury compensated).

## 19. BNDES export-service loans to contractors abroad (Odebrecht; Mariel port, Angola)
- **Years:** 2003-2015. **Government:** Lula 1-2, Dilma.
- **Size:** BNDES disbursed US$ 10.5 bn for service exports; five contractors took 98%, Odebrecht 76% (Crusoe via https://crusoe.com.br/?p=29855). Odebrecht was third largest recipient of total BNDES disbursements 2003-2018, R$ 51.3 bn (IPCA-updated) (https://www.bndes.gov.br/arquivos/apresentacao-odebrecht.pdf ; includes domestic). Mariel: US$ 682-692 m, with Brazil's loans partly on a non-repayable basis of up to US$ 107 m reported by one outlet (https://www.congressoemfoco.com.br/noticias/ministerio-confirma-gasto-a-fundo-perdido-para-cuba ); another source says total cost above US$ 900 m. Angola: about US$ 4 bn estimated vs US$ 3.27 bn in contracts (Aos Fatos, https://www.aosfatos.org/noticias/e-falso-que-pt-doou-r-14-bilhoes-do-bndes-para-governo-de-angola/) vs US$ 2.33 bn in BNDES data 2009-2014. Sources disagree.
- **Mechanism:** BNDES-Exim; foreign states bear repayment risk, guaranteed by the Treasury-backed export guarantee fund (background; UNVERIFIED).
- **After:** secrecy ("caixa-preta") fight; CGU/TCU probes; Odebrecht plea deals (Lava Jato).

## 20. Sete Brasil (drilling-rig company for pre-salt)
- **Years:** 2010-2016. **Government:** Lula 2, Dilma 1.
- **Transferred:** Petrobras and state pension funds and banks capitalised a company to order 28 rigs from new Brazilian yards (local-content rules); Petrobras cut order to 14 (https://eixos.com.br/a-saga-da-recuperacao-judicial-da-sete-brasil/). BNDES financing never released.
- **After:** judicial recovery filed 29 April 2016; debts reported R$ 14 bn, R$ 17.4 bn, over R$ 19 bn (differing dates); federal government largest creditor (two-thirds) (https://istoe.com.br/governo-e-o-maior-credor-da-sete-brasil ; https://www.tribunapr.com.br/noticias/economia/sete-brasil-vai-pedir-recuperacao-judicial/). Petrobras booked R$ 5.6 bn loss in 2016. Corruption replicated (government material). FGTS lost R$ 798 m (https://eixos.com.br/energia/caixa-deve-apurar-responsabilidade-por-prejuizo-de-r-798-milhoes-causado-pela-sete-brasil-e-estaleiros-a-fundo-de-garantia/). Costs: pension funds (Funcef, Petros), FGTS, Treasury.

## Patterns
1. **Public money, private control:** the state (BNDES/Treasury, FMM, pension funds Petros/Funcef, FGTS, Eletrobras) carries the capital and risk; private groups (JBS, EBX, Odebrecht, Oi owners, Votorantim) hold control and upside. Losses or defaults land on BNDES, the Treasury, pension funds (cases 1-4, 6, 19, 20).
2. **Concentration in "champions" plus political-finance link:** a handful of firms receive most of the money (Odebrecht 76% of export-services financing; five contractors 98%), and the same contractors form a cartel at Petrobras and pay 1-3% kickbacks to parties (cases 5, 11, 19). Dozens of projects (Belo Monte, Madeira, Sete Brasil) show the same cast.
3. **Cost shifted onto the weak, benefit onto the strong:** Indigenous and riverine people, workers and the Amazon bear the social-environmental bill while consultation and land rules are bent through decrees, provisional measures and legislative shortcuts (Belo Monte decree without consultation, Terra Legal, Forest Code). Estimates of cost and damage diverge sharply (BNDES vs TCU, MPF vs government), so the study should always show both.
4. **Legal rewriting to fit a deal:** decrees, MPs and statutes changed just before a deal (Oi/BrT PGO, MP 458, MP 579, Lei 12.276 cessao onerosa).
5. **Aggressive bids then distress:** auction premiums (airports 160-673%) or inflated contracts later ended in relicitation, judicial recovery and renegotiation (Viracopos, highways, OGX, Oi, Sete Brasil).
6. **Fiscal opacity:** subsidy cost estimates vary by orders of magnitude (R$ 44.5 bn to R$ 180.7 bn; R$ 11.6-15.9 bn in Ipea), and tax relief (desoneracao, R$ 77.9 bn in 5 years) lacked clear job results.
