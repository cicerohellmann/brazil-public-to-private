Every term of power, every deal and disaster, and the data on public patrimony, wages and wealth, on one year axis from 1822 to today.

How to read it: each row shares the same years. Pick a year (hover, tap or drag the slider); the red line marks it in every chart, and the panel below lists who was in power, the deals signed and the disasters that year. The link in the address bar opens on that year.
