How to read it

  • Change since a year: every line starts at 0% in the year you pick under "Compare with". Up = it grew since then.
  • Real values: one small chart per unit, so % and R$ never share an axis.
  • Dashed line = not verified yet. It has a source, but no second check.
  • A break in a line = no data that year (the household survey skipped 2000 and 2010).
  • Bands along the top = who was president. Deals and disasters for the year are in the box next to the chart.
  • A deal or disaster in the same year as a line moving is order in time, not proof of cause.
