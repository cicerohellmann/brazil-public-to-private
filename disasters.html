Main corrections applied

  • Serra Pelada / Marabá: "3 official dead, 60+ claimed" belongs to the 1987 Marabá bridge protest, not the mine.
  • Brumadinho: asset freeze is R$ 11 bn (no source for R$ 16 bn); tailings about 12 million m3; one headline said 242 dead but its body says 272.
  • Rio Verde 2001: at least 2 dead and 3 missing, 8 km, 30 ha (not 5 dead).
  • Mariana 2024 deal: R$ 170 bn split as R$ 100 bn public, R$ 32 bn Samarco, R$ 8 bn Indigenous and traditional peoples.
  • Angra 1: official admission 30 Jan 2023; the 90 litres is the company's own estimate.
  • REPAR: R$ 610 million is a court-ordered minimum compensation, not a fine; the settlement was about R$ 1.4 bn.
  • Vila Socó: the "11 dead" figure was wrong; official count 93, other counts up to 508.
  • Pau d'Arco: no convictions. The "155 police, two convicted" line is Eldorado dos Carajás.
  • Petrópolis: 876 displaced and 5 missing are Petrópolis figures, not Niterói.
  • Madeira dams: the auctions were 2007 and 2008 and included state-owned partners (Furnas, Chesf, Eletrosul), so "private concession" needs qualifying.
  • Endosulfan: no 12 Sept 2010 ban; Anvisa RDC 28/2010 set a phase-out ending 2013-2014.
