Fernando Henrique Cardoso held office in 2 terms: 1995–1999, 1999–2003.

Who got richer, who got poorer

Per term, start to end, inflation removed:

  • 1995–1999: household income per person +3%, real minimum wage +26%, survey Gini 61.5 → 61.4.
  • 1999–2003: household income per person -3%, real minimum wage +17%, survey Gini 61.4 → 60.3.

Each term has its own table in its section. Surveys miss top incomes; tax data catches them, so the two gap measures can move apart.

How they came and left

1995–1999: Direct election 1994: 54.28% in the 1st round. Left: end of term.; 1999–2003: Direct election 1998: 53.06% in the 1st round (after the 1997 constitutional amendment allowing re-election). Left: end of term.

The biggest facts

  • 29 deals signed in these terms in the ledger. Roles (started, continued, reversed) are not classified yet.
  • 7 later disasters are linked to these deals, with 328 deaths recorded. A link is not proof of cause.
  • 26 decision-makers are mapped to these terms in the people files.
  • Real minimum wage went from R$ 542 (1994) to R$ 797 (2002), in R$ of Sep 2026 per month. This is the situation, not a score.
