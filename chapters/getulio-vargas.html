Getúlio Vargas, 1930–1934

President. Head of the Provisional Government after the Revolution of 1930 (armed seizure of power; Júlio Prestes won the 1930 vote with 57.68%). Left: end of term.

Ruled by decree without a Congress; 'provisional' until the Constituent Assembly. The Constituent Assembly elected him on 17 Jul 1934; the new constitution took effect 16 Jul 1934. Boundary date 20 Jul 1934 is approximate.

President. Self-coup of 10 Nov 1937 (Estado Novo); no elections held. Left: coup.

Congress closed; 1937 Constitution gave the president full power; deposed by the Army on 29 Oct 1945.
