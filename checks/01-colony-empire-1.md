## Biggest corrections
1. Loronha: the "400-500%" and "20,000 quintals" are in Wikipedia (not uncited); renewal was 1506 to 1512 then Bixorda, Crown took over 1515.
2. Captaincies: the 16 private captaincies left in 1750s included Madeira, Azores, Cabo Verde, Príncipe, not just Brazil.
3. Diamonds: sixth contract starts 1762 in one source; Brant's third contract varies 1748-1755; "12 Aug 1771" comes only from CartaCapital.
4. Banco do Brasil: the 2% commission and tobacco are not supported; the bank's real problem was the Crown owing it 4.8 million (twice capital). Capital 1,200 contos in 1,200 shares.
5. "Law 641" of 1852 is Decreto 641 in the official database; Lei 601 is case 13, not 14.
6. Companhia de Pernambuco: filled gap, 49,344 enslaved Angola to Brazil 1761-1786; liquidation to 1813 unconfirmed.
7. Morro Velho 1960 sale rests on one press article; Wikipedia says 1957 control change.
8. Not reached (unconfirmable in this pass): telegraph, City Improvements 1862 date, Bahia and Pernambuco rail guarantees, capitação rate, 241,405 carats, the 1801 alvará date.
