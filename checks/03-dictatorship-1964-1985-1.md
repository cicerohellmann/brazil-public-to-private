# Fact-check: notes/03-dictatorship-1964-1985.md (all 16 cases)

Method: pages opened with WebFetch; PDFs saved and read with pdftotext. "Opened" = I read the page or PDF text. "Search only" = a search result summary, page not opened (blocked, 403/429 or binary). Notes file not edited.

Biggest corrections: (1) Case 4 US$88.5 mn and "CPT 1,000 victims" not found in the cited pages; the two court outcomes do not conflict. (2) Case 8 the US$2 bn tariff subsidy is Pinto's own estimate, not Eliezer Batista's figure. (3) Case 9 Alcoa/Billiton did not "enter" MRN via Cruz Alta; MRN bought it from them. (4) Case 16 "11 dead" at Vila Socó is wrong (11 is the P-36 platform toll). (5) Case 2 Amoroso debt is US$400 mn, not 415. (6) Case 11 the 150,000/5,000 migrants figure is not in the cited page.
