## Biggest corrections (summary)
1. Sete Brasil loss: R$ 798 m "FGTS" is really US$ 798 m in the shipbuilding guarantee fund (FGCN).
2. Terra Legal "33% irregular" is the Rondonia audit only (about 21,000 ha), article from Dec 2021.
3. Terra Legal 2,500 ha ceiling came from Congress's amendment in the conversion law (Lei 13.465/2017), not from the MP 759 text.
4. Garcia's R$ 414 bn is the BNDES share of the ~10%-of-GDP total to public banks.
5. Forest concession R$ 7 m/yr cited to a sedep.com.br page that is unrelated; unsupported. "Three firms" in Jamari was three areas; five firms bid.
6. Cunha's R$ 10 bn is MP 591 (transmission indemnities), source is Agencia Camara 3 Dec 2012, not InfoMoney.
7. Mariel: US$ 107 m is the Proex figure for 2010-2013; MDIC refuses to give the total.
