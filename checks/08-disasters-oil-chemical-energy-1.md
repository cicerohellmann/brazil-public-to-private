# Fact-check: 08-disasters-oil-chemical-energy.html

Checked 2026-11-01. Notes file not edited. "Opened" = fetched with WebFetch and the page text supported the value. "Search only" = the value appeared only in a search result summary, not on a page I opened (weaker). Some cited URLs returned 402/403/404 or changed content; those are listed as such.

Top corrections (details below):
1. Angra 1: the 11 Oct 2022 "admission" conflicts with the 30 Jan 2023 official admission (Poder360, Movimento Econômico). The page contradicts itself (11 Oct vs "4 months").
2. REPAR: R$ 610 million is a 2019 court minimum, not a fine; the real outcome is a ~R$ 1.4 billion settlement (late 2021), of which ~R$ 900 million+ sits in the state fund.
3. Amapá: Starboard/Perfin hold Gemini (80/20); LMTE is 85% Gemini; Gemini was bought by Energisa in Feb 2022. Gemini bought Isolux's asset at end of 2019 and took control Jan 2020. LMTE concession was originally Isolux's (decree 8 Oct 2008).
4. REDUC: Ibama fines total R$ 51.05 million (three fines), not R$ 35 million + R$ 15 million.
5. Ultracargo: omits the Cetesb fine of R$ 22.5 million (15 Apr 2015); deal is a TAC with MPF and MPSP; amount R$ 67.3 million (one source says 67.5).
6. Vila Socó: warning came from Petrobras president Ueki to Governor Montoro, not "an engineer"; fire was in the early hours of 25 Feb per Agência Pública (24 Feb per Alesp).
7. Frade: commerciality was declared 6 Aug 1998, so "1999 concession round" cannot be right; stakes are Chevron 51.74%, Petrobras 30%, Frade Japão 18.26%. The R$ 311 million TAC covers two leaks (Nov 2011 and Mar 2012).
8. Cidade dos Meninos: factory ran about 1950 to about 1960/61, not 1965.
9. 2001 rationing: R$ 45.2 billion is in present-day values; in 2001 money it was R$ 32.2 billion (R$ 19.681 billion passed to consumers). The FGV "2% of GDP / R$ 60 billion" figure could not be found.
