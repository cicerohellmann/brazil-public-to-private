## Summary of main corrections

1. Pau D'Arco: "155 police / two convicted" is the Eldorado dos Carajás figure; Pau D'Arco had no conviction as of 2022 and in May 2026 the TJPA sent 16 of 17 accused to jury. The release (18-19 Dec) came before the STJ order (22 Dec).
2. Petrópolis: 876 displaced and 5 missing are Petrópolis figures, not Niterói.
3. Pantanal: 3.9 million ha is not in the cited analyses (4.5 million ha, 44,998 km2). "Mostly private" is 152 of about 286 ignition points; the MP says intent cannot be proven from images.
4. Madeira: auctions were 10 Dec 2007 (Santo Antônio) and 19 May 2008 (Jirau). Santo Antônio was led by state-owned Furnas (39%), so "private concession" needs qualifying.
5. Chapada do Apodi: the cited PDF does not contain 97% / 60% / 46 samples; Zé Maria killed April 2010. STF upheld the Ceará ban unanimously on 26 May 2023.
6. Belo Monte: ownership in the note is the 2010 snapshot; later split gives the Eletrobras group 49.98% plus Petros and Funcef 10% each. MPF case now has a June 2026 filing with 635 families.
7. Algodões I: it is a state dam (COMDEPI/Piauí), the state was held liable by court sentence.
8. Rio Verde: 2013 school case has a March 2018 first-instance award of R$ 150,000 (appealed).
