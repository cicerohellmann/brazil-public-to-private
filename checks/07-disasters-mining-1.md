# Fact-check: 07-disasters-mining.md (all cases)

Method: each URL opened with WebFetch, or a replacement source opened. "Search only" means I saw it in a search result but could not open a page that states it; treat it as unconfirmed for public use. Today: 2026-11-01. Notes file not edited.

Biggest corrections in one view:
1. Serra Pelada: the "official 3 dead / miners claim 60+" belongs to the 1987 Marabá bridge protest, not the mine. A "1983 collapse killed 17-19" was not found.
2. Brumadinho: R$ 16 billion asset freeze not found anywhere (R$ 11 bn is). The "20 times" finding was not confirmed (page blocked). Congresso em Foco headline says 242 but its body says 272.
3. Rio Verde (2001): deaths are "at least 2 dead, 3 missing" (not 5); stream 8 km, 30 ha (not 6.4 km / 43 ha).
4. Hydro 2018: IBAMA fine R$ 20 million confirmed (2 x R$ 10 m). R$ 250 million civil action was filed by the Pará government (not the MP-PA). Vale-until-2011 confirmed (Hydro bought Vale's aluminium unit 28 Feb 2011).
5. Mariana: IBAMA R$ 250 m was five fines of R$ 50 m (confirmed); R$ 350.7 m is 25 notices (confirmed). Tailings volume and river length are not settled by the sources I could open.
6. Cataguases: volume 500 million litres per the MPF complaint (Migalhas), not 1.2 billion; "600,000 people" not found.
7. Imerys "bought from Vale in 2011": not supported. The 2011 Vale sale was the aluminium business to Hydro. Needs separate check.
8. Braskem 2023 settlement R$ 1.7 bn: confirmed 21 Jul 2023. Dutch case: sources conflict on outcome.
9. Mariana 2024 breakdown: R$ 170 bn / signed 25 Oct / STF 6 Nov 2024 confirmed. R$ 38 bn spent, 20 years: not found in the opened page; O Fator gives different parts (R$ 100 bn public, R$ 32 bn Samarco, R$ 8 bn Indigenous/traditional).
