# Fact-check: 04-privatization-1985-2002.md, first half (cases 1-12)

Checked 2026-11-01. Notes file not edited. Scope: sections 1 to 12 (the first 12 of the 25 "## " sections; section 13 onward belongs to the second half).
Status: confirmed / corrected / contradicted / unconfirmable. "Opened" = fetched in full or read after download. "Snippet only" = seen in a search summary, not opened (flagged).
Review needed by the lead: items marked **ACTION**.

Key corrections at a glance
- Garcia's -62.4% is the WHOLE steel sector (Setor), not "flat steel" (Planos is -59.8%).
- Vale price: R$3.338 bn is about US$3.0 bn, not "US$3.3 bn" (Congresso em Foco says "cerca de US$ 3 bilhoes"; English Wikipedia mislabels it US$3.338 bn).
- Usiminas price: Garcia/Schmidt US$1.49 bn vs contemporaneous CSM US$1.2 bn (14% over minimum). Unreconciled.
- Telebras ágio: Teletime 63.74% (arithmetic checks out); BNDES page says 53.74% (looks like a typo). Minimum R$13.47 bn is consistent with the per-company table.
- Eletropaulo: sold AT the minimum price (eumed + pt-Wikipedia table "2,026 bilhoes pelo valor minimo"). The "several hundred million above" line is a pre-auction expectation.
- Embraer: R$154 m and US$182.4 m are consistent with each other at the Dec 1994 exchange rate (about R$0.85/US$, my arithmetic). Stakes quoted from DIEESE are later total-capital shares, not the auction result.
- Light: BCB 1996 report supports about US$2.3-2.4 bn; the US$1.7 bn (English Wikipedia Light page) is the outlier.
- Brasilcom and Arthur D. Little did NOT win one contract together: two separate BNDES services (Teletime).
- Vale "BNDES subsidised financing": the cited CUT article does not say it. Only a jus.com.br article (secondary) and an advocacy op-ed (R$2 bn of R$3.3 bn) do.
