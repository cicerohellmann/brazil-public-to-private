Counter-view: this was a Bolivian sovereign lease (Bolivia's own attempt to assert control over a territory it could not police, where Brazilians were already the settlers); Brazil's buy-out was paid to end a foreign-run charter, not a private-to-public giveaway. Also, Acre was largely settled by Brazilian rubber tappers, which is Brazil's legal-moral case (Wikipedia Acre War page).

## Patterns / gaps
- Pattern 1 claim "Ford sold at a fraction of cost" is not a public-to-private transfer; Ford lost money. Also Docas had no interest guarantee. These weaken pattern 1.
- "Bernardes veto" in pattern 4 is, per Rogers 1959, a Minas state official's opposition.
- Largest corrections: Docas term (39 years then extended); Lumber land (15 km maximum, 324,800 ha found); Fordlandia sale price (US$244,200 per Al Jazeera, loss figure uncertain); Pinheiros reversal (restricted in 1992, not fully halted); Petrobras monopoly (1995 amendment); Law 990's 15m pounds was a ceiling.
